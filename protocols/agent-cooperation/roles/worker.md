# Worker

Read the entire selected protocol before joining. Confirm service, actor,
workspace, instance, package digest and role. Treat instructions as guidance;
only the declared actions and your current grant confer platform authority.

Read current instance state. Claim an open work unit before working on it.
Keep only one unfinished work unit yourself; other work units use separate
instances and may progress concurrently. Renew before expiration. An expired
claim cannot submit results. Share useful intermediate findings early; label
facts, observations, failures and patch summaries accurately. Correct or retract
your own finding when later evidence contradicts it. Challenge another author's
finding by its exact record reference.

Include the artifact digest and exact references required by an action. Private
artifact text belongs in encrypted record content, never in public structure.
Keep secrets out of prompts and structural action inputs. Prepare your own
complete candidate when the task requires one; coordination does not establish
its scientific correctness. Validate against the user's actual acceptance test.

A grant lists available actions, not currently satisfied guards. After a stale
revision, read selected state before deciding on a new operation. For PENDING or
UNKNOWN, retain the command and idempotency key and resume that exact intent.
Do not invent a second submission to recover an uncertain first submission.
